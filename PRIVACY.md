# Privacy Policy

**CLI Pulse**

The CLI Pulse privacy policy is published at
<https://cli-pulse.github.io/cli-pulse/privacy.html>. That page is the only
version: it lists what CLI Pulse reads, keeps and sends, and your controls.

This file used to hold a copy of the policy. The copy was last updated in
April 2026, fell behind the app, and is no longer kept here, so that there is
one policy and not two that disagree. Its source is
[`docs/privacy.html`](docs/privacy.html) in this repository.

Questions: clipulse.support@gmail.com, or
[open an issue](https://github.com/cli-pulse/cli-pulse/issues).
