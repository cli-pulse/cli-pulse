Frequently asked questions

What is CLI Pulse?

A developer app for monitoring usage, quotas, resets, sessions, and cost estimates across the AI coding providers and CLI tools you already use.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch are available on the App Store. Android is available via Google Play internal testing. Windows and Linux are not supported.

Does any data leave my device?

Your provider API keys, session cookies, OAuth tokens, and raw session-log contents stay on your device. CLI Pulse syncs only aggregated usage metrics, quota summaries, cost estimates, session display metadata, and device heartbeat information to your account.

Are cost numbers the same as my provider bill?

No. CLI Pulse estimates cost locally from each provider's published pricing and your recorded token or request counts. Provider invoices remain the authoritative source.

How do I get support?

Email clipulse.support@gmail.com for general support, or open an issue on GitHub Issues for bug reports and feature requests.
