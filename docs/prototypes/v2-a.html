Frequently asked questions

What is CLI Pulse?

A developer app for monitoring usage, quotas, resets, sessions, and cost estimates across AI coding providers.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS, iOS, watchOS are available on the App Store. Android is in beta via Google Play internal testing.

Does any data leave my device?

API keys, cookies, OAuth tokens, and raw session logs stay local. Only aggregated metrics and metadata sync to your account.
